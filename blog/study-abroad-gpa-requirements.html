What GPA Do You Need to Study Abroad?

6 min read · Updated October 2026

Before you get to cost or credit questions, most study abroad programs have a gate you have to clear first: a minimum GPA. Here's the typical range, and what happens if your academic standing is shakier than the requirement.

The typical range

There is no national standard; each school and program sets its own. Published minimums commonly sit around 2.0 to 3.0. UMBC and Suffolk University both require at least a 2.5, and both note that some programs set a higher bar; the University of Nebraska Omaha lists a 2.0 to 2.5 minimum depending on the program. Competitive exchange programs with limited spots can expect more. Treat any number you see elsewhere as a reference point, not your program's actual requirement.

Academic probation and study abroad don't mix well

This is where a lot of students get caught off guard. Many schools require good academic standing, which rules out anyone currently on probation: UMBC and the University of Nebraska Omaha both state that students on academic or judicial/disciplinary probation are not eligible, and Suffolk requires good academic, disciplinary and financial standing. If you're on probation and hoping to study abroad, ask your education abroad office early what your school allows.

The requirement doesn't necessarily end at acceptance. UMBC requires good standing both when you apply and immediately before your term abroad, and Suffolk says admitted students who fall out of good standing may be barred from participating. A rough semester after you're accepted can still affect your eligibility.

If you don't currently meet the minimum

Some schools have an exception route. UMBC tells students below the GPA minimum to contact its Education Abroad Office about special approval procedures, and Nebraska Omaha grants approval case by case. Others, like Suffolk, state that admission decisions are final. Ask your office whether an exception process exists rather than assuming either way.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the typical minimum GPA for study abroad? There is no single standard; published minimums commonly fall between 2.0 and 3.0 (2.5 at UMBC and Suffolk, 2.0–2.5 at Nebraska Omaha depending on the program). Check your specific program's requirement.

Can you study abroad while on academic probation? At many schools, no — UMBC and Nebraska Omaha both exclude students on academic or disciplinary probation. Check your school's policy.

Does my GPA need to stay above the minimum the whole time I'm abroad? Often until departure, at least — UMBC checks good standing at application and again immediately before the term abroad, and Suffolk may bar admitted students who fall out of good standing.

What if I don't meet the minimum GPA requirement? Ask about an exception process. UMBC has special approval procedures for students below its minimum and Nebraska Omaha decides case by case, while Suffolk treats decisions as final.
