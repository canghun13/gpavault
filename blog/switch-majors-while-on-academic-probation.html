Can You Switch Majors While on Academic Probation?

6 min read · July 2026
Advertisement · Google AdSense

Generally, yes — and at many schools, an academic advisor might even recommend it, on the theory that a low GPA is sometimes a sign of being in the wrong major rather than a lack of effort. But "generally yes" comes with real conditions worth understanding before you assume it's a clean fix.

The conditions that actually matter

  • Minimum GPA to declare. Some departments require a minimum GPA to formally declare their major, which can itself be an obstacle while you're on probation. Some schools handle this with a conditional or provisional declaration; others require you to clear probation first.
  • "Viable path" review. At several schools, a change-of-major agreement while on probation specifically requires the receiving department to believe the new major represents a genuine path back to good academic standing — not simply an escape from a difficult subject.
  • It doesn't erase your GPA. Switching majors changes what future coursework counts toward, but your cumulative GPA — the actual basis of your probation status — doesn't reset. You still need to raise it above your school's threshold.

Talk to your advisor first

At many schools, an advisor consultation is a required step in the change-of-major process specifically for students on probation, not just a suggestion. Come prepared with a specific reason the new major fits better — genuine interest, stronger performance in related coursework, or a clearer sense of the career path — rather than framing it purely as an escape from your current struggles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will switching majors automatically get me off academic probation? No — probation is based on your cumulative GPA, which switching majors doesn't erase. You still need to raise your GPA above your school's threshold regardless of major.

Do I need a minimum GPA to declare a new major? Some departments do. Check your target major's specific requirements — some schools allow conditional declaration while others require the threshold first.

Will switching majors while on probation delay my graduation? It depends on how much coursework carries over. Use the Major Switch Cost Calculator to estimate your specific case.

Should I talk to my advisor before deciding? Yes — at many schools this is a required step, since departments often need to believe the new major represents a viable path back to good standing.
