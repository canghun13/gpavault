Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy explains what information gpavault.com ("GPA Vault", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

What you enter into calculators. All calculations run in your browser. Grades, loan amounts, test scores and other figures you type are not sent to or stored on our servers.

Email. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply and to fix any problem you report.

Usage data. Our analytics and advertising partners collect information automatically, described below.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, such as which pages are viewed, how long visits last, and what kind of device and browser is used. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects data such as your approximate location and device information. We use this only in aggregate to improve the site. You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting your data with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized based on your browsing.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. GPA Vault itself does not set cookies to identify you; the cookies on this site are set by Google Analytics and Google AdSense as described above. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The calculators work with cookies blocked.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone other than the service providers named in this policy.

Children's privacy

GPA Vault is intended for high school and college students and their families. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

Pages link to outside sources such as StudentAid.gov and college websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
